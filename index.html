FAQs

Questions answered.

Do I need to sign a long contract?
No. Our agreements are month-to-month. Pause or stop any time.
What counts as a lead?
A verified contact with name, phone or email, and stated intent to buy or enquire.
What if I get a bad lead?
We have a lead quality guarantee. Duplicates or invalid contacts are not charged.
How do leads reach me?
By email, SMS, WhatsApp, or direct CRM integration — whichever you prefer.
Can I control the volume?
Yes. You set a monthly lead cap. We never exceed it without your approval.
Which ad platforms do you use?
Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads, and TikTok depending on your industry and audience.
